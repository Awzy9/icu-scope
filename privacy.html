Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

ICU Scope iOS app

The app does not collect, track or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or third-party tracking tools.

  • The app downloads the public article feed from icu-scope.vercel.app. Like any web request, this lets our host see your IP address and device type in standard server logs.
  • Articles you save, your collections, notes, reading log, keyword watchlist, flashcards, settings, and the last downloaded feed are stored only on your device so the app works offline. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app, or using “Clear saved articles and cache” in Settings, removes them. Please do not put patient information in notes.
  • If you tap “Read full text” on an open-access article, the app downloads that article from Europe PMC (europepmc.org, run by EMBL-EBI), which can see your IP address and device type like any web host. The article is then kept on your device for offline reading.
  • Optional features run on your device: the daily reminder is a local notification, “Listen” uses the iPhone’s built-in speech voices, and “Lock with Face ID” uses iOS to check it is you. The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Tapping “Open source” or a link opens the publisher’s site in your browser. That site has its own privacy policy.

ICU Scope website

  • Analytics: the website uses PostHog to count page views and clicks (for example which sections are opened). Session recording is turned off. This applies to the website only, not the app.
  • Optional sign-in: the website offers a sign-in for a shared account. If you use it, your sign-in details are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Your browser: preferences such as theme and bookmarks are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device.

Article content and AI summaries

Article details come from PubMed and public critical-care sources. Some summaries are generated automatically by AI and can contain errors. ICU Scope is an educational reading aid for healthcare professionals and students. It does not give medical advice. Always check the original source and follow your local protocols.

Children

ICU Scope is intended for healthcare professionals and students and is not directed at children.
