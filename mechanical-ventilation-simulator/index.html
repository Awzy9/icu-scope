      📋 Clinical cases

      Multi-step cases that combine setting the simulator above to a lung-protective starting point with decision points as the (simulated) patient evolves. Non-blocking — you can continue past a missed step — but feedback checks your actual live settings, not just a quiz answer.

      Educational use only. Simplified physiology model for teaching — not validated for clinical use and not a substitute for bedside assessment, training, or institutional protocols.

      Alerts and scenario notes are anchored to major society guidance (ATS/ESICM/SCCM, Surviving Sepsis Campaign) and landmark trials (ARDSnet, Amato 2015, PROSEVA, EXPRESS, ART, HOT-ICU/LOCO₂) where cited inline — always verify against the primary literature before drawing clinical conclusions.
