Support

ICU Scope is a daily-updated feed of ICU and critical-care literature, available as a website and an iOS app.

Contact us

Report a bug, request a feature or ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Please don’t include personal or patient information.

Common questions

How often is it updated?
The feed refreshes automatically every day. Pull down on the Today or Browse tab to reload.

Does it work offline?
Yes. The app keeps the last downloaded articles and everything you saved.

Where are my saved articles?
On your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

An article looks wrong or a link is broken.
Open an issue with the article title and we will look into it.

Are the summaries medical advice?
No. They are generated automatically as a reading aid and can contain errors. Always read the source paper.
